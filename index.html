Before you meet

Common questions.

Practical answers about meeting me. For anything else, call or write.

How do I book a consultation?

Use the “Book a consultation” form above. It opens WhatsApp with your request already written, addressed to me. I confirm the date and time personally. You can also call +91 97171 95907 or email advmanojchowdhary@rediff.com.

Can we meet by video or phone?

Yes. Meetings can be in person at chambers, by video call or by phone. Choose the format when you book.

Where are the chambers?

Chamber 312, 3rd Floor, M.C. Setalvad Block, in front of ‘D’ Gate, Supreme Court of India, New Delhi. There are also offices in New Ashok Nagar, Delhi and Sector 15, Noida. Each office card above has a “Get directions” link.

What should I bring to the first meeting?

Bring copies of the documents that relate to the matter, such as notices, orders, agreements and correspondence, along with a short timeline of events. This makes the first meeting far more useful.

Does contacting you make me a client?

No. Reading this website or sending an enquiry does not create an advocate–client relationship. That begins only once a matter is formally taken up.

About

An advocate with a decade inside technology.

I am an advocate practising before the Supreme Court of India and an associate at JSAS, the law firm of Jayprakash Somani Advocates & Solicitors, New Delhi. I hold a B.Tech, an MBA in Finance and an LL.B.

Before joining the bar, I managed technology programmes for global clients, including ten years as a Senior Manager at HCL Technologies. My practice combines litigation with advisory work in corporate law, cyber law, intellectual property, mergers and acquisitions, and arbitration.

Technology disputes are decided on facts that few lawyers have seen from the inside.

Where law meets technology.

Many disputes and transactions turn on how a system was built, configured or operated. My engineering background lets me read those facts directly.

Technical disputesFailed projects, outages, data loss and cyber incidents, where logs and electronic records are the core evidence.

JSAS — Jayprakash Somani Advocates & Solicitors

JSAS (JSAS Supreme Legal LLP) is a New Delhi law firm focused on practice before the Supreme Court of India. It was founded by Adv. Jayprakash Somani, who has practised since 1989 and has written more than 65 books on law. The firm handles appeals, special leave petitions and writ petitions, along with matters before High Courts and national tribunals, and runs regular legal webinars through NPLC.

I work with the firm on Supreme Court and tribunal matters, bringing technology, corporate and cyber law work together with its appellate practice.
